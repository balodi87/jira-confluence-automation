# Create Quiz Workflow

## Purpose
Generate objective, multiple-choice technical quizzes with clear answer keys and concise explanations to evaluate conceptual mastery.

## Standards & Constraints
- **Format**: Markdown (`.md`).
- **Question Structure**:
  - Numbered question prompt with clear, unambiguous phrasing.
  - Exactly 4 options labeled `A)`, `B)`, `C)`, `D)`.
  - Exactly 1 correct answer.
- **Answer Key & Explanation**:
  - Provide an expandable or separated `## Answer Key` section.
  - Include 1–2 sentence explanation for why the correct answer is right and why distractors are wrong.
- **Tone & Style**: Direct, professional, and educational. Avoid trivia or overly pedantic edge cases.
- **Length & Quantity Constraint**: Quizzes must contain strictly and exactly 5 questions per set.
