# Jira/Confluence Automation Ideas

## 1. Weekly Project Status Report

**Problem it solves:**
Managers often spend time collecting updates from Jira, checking progress, and preparing a weekly status report manually. This automation would create a consistent summary of project progress and publish it to Confluence.

**Data it needs:**
- Jira projects, boards, or filters to include
- Issues completed, in progress, and overdue during the reporting period
- Issue status, priority, assignee, and due date
- Sprint or release information
- Blockers and risks recorded in Jira
- Confluence page or template where the report should be published

## 2. Overdue Work and Blocker Alerts

**Problem it solves:**
Managers may not notice delayed tasks or blockers until they affect a milestone. This automation would identify risks early and notify the responsible people or publish an alert in Confluence.

**Data it needs:**
- Jira issues and their due dates
- Current issue status and last update time
- Priority and project milestone information
- Assignee and team details
- Linked blockers, dependencies, and comments
- Notification recipients or a Confluence page for the alerts

## 3. Project Decision and Action-Item Tracker

**Problem it solves:**
Important decisions and follow-up actions can become scattered across Jira comments, Confluence pages, and meeting notes. This automation would collect decisions and action items, assign owners, and track completion.

**Data it needs:**
- Confluence meeting notes and project documentation
- Jira issues, comments, and labels related to decisions or actions
- Action-item owner, due date, and status
- Project or team identifiers
- Links between Jira issues and Confluence pages
- A destination Confluence page or report template
